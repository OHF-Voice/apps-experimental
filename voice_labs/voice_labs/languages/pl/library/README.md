# The command library (Polish)

Przykładowe polecenia do biblioteki interfejsu (Polish), as the English
library (voice_labs/languages/en/library/): titles and needs are
the UI's, in English; descriptions are said in hints ("Wygląda na to, że
chcesz <description>, ..."), so each is what one wants to do, in the
infinitive: "włączyć światło".

Keywords are lowercase with their diacritics, in the forms they are said:
verbs in the imperative ("włącz", "zgaś") and the infinitive after "możesz"
("włączyć"), nouns in the case the verb takes ("lampę", "światła"). The
prepositions ("w", "na", "do") and politeness ("proszę", "czy możesz") are
filler (the language pack's); a name is heard in any case ("w łazience" for
the Łazienka: adapt-ctc's endings).

A response says a device's state after its name, agreeing with it: its
gender is guessed from the name's first word ("Lampa stojąca włączona",
"Wentylator włączony", "Radio włączone", "Drzwi wejściowe zablokowane").
